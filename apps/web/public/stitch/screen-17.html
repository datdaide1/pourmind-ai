Friday Night Tropical Mix

Session Start

I'm hosting a small gathering tonight. We want something tropical, but not overly sweet. I have a decent aged Jamaican rum, Campari, and some fresh pineapples I can juice. What can we make?

local_bar

That’s an excellent combination. With aged Jamaican rum, Campari, and fresh pineapple juice, you have the exact foundation for a modern classic: The Jungle Bird.

It perfectly balances the rich funk of the rum with the tropical sweetness of the pineapple, anchored by the distinct bitter bite of Campari. It's sophisticated but undeniably fun.

star Featured Recipe

Jungle Bird

  • Aged Jamaican Rum 1.5 oz
  • Campari 0.75 oz
  • Fresh Pineapple Juice 1.5 oz
  • Fresh Lime Juice 0.5 oz
  • Demerara Syrup 0.5 oz

That sounds perfect. One guest prefers something a bit lighter and less bitter than Campari. What's a good alternative using similar ingredients?

local_bar

If we want to pull back on the bitterness but keep the tropical rum profile, we can pivot towards a classic Mai Tai variation, or simply modify the Jungle Bird.

To modify the Jungle Bird for a softer palate: swap the Campari for Aperol. It maintains the beautiful color and citrus notes, but halves the perceived bitterness while adding a touch more sweetness.
