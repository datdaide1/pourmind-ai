The Smoked Old Fashioned

Strong • 32% ABV 5 Mins Prep Smoky Complex

Ingredients

  • Kentucky Bourbon 2 oz
  • Simple Syrup 0.25 oz
  • Angostura Bitters 2 dashes
  • Orange Peel Garnish
  • Cherry Wood Chips For smoke
warning

Safety Check

Contains high ABV. Ensure proper ventilation when using the smoking gun. No common allergens.

Preparation

1

Prepare the Glass

Place a large, clear ice cube into a heavy-bottomed rocks glass. Let it sit for a minute to chill the glass slightly while you mix.

2

Mix Ingredients

In a mixing glass, combine the bourbon, simple syrup, and bitters. Add ice and stir gently for about 20-30 seconds until well chilled and properly diluted.

3

Strain & Smoke

Strain the mixture into your prepared rocks glass over the large ice cube. Use a smoking gun with cherry wood chips to infuse the drink with smoke. Cover with a glass cloche for 30 seconds.

4

Garnish

Express the oils from a fresh orange peel over the surface of the drink. Wipe the rim of the glass with the peel, then drop it in alongside a premium cocktail cherry.

Mixologist Notes

Chef Julian, Lead Bartender

"The key to a perfect smoked old fashioned is balance. Don't overdo the smoke; a gentle infusion of cherry wood elevates the caramel notes of the bourbon without overpowering it. Always stir, never shake."
